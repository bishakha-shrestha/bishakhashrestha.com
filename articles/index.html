Learning Resources for High School Students

Explore educational materials across various scientific fields.

Machine Learning

Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence (AI) and computer science which focuses on the use of data and algorithms to imitate the way that humans learn, gradually improving its accuracy.

  • Google's Machine Learning Crash Course - A fast-paced, practical introduction to machine learning.
  • Kaggle: Intro to Machine Learning - Learn the core ideas of machine learning and build your first models.
  • Fast.ai - Making neural nets uncool again (accessible to everyone).
  • 3Blue1Brown: Neural Networks - Visual introduction to the math behind neural networks.

Big Data

Big data refers to data sets that are too large or complex to be dealt with by traditional data-processing application software.

  • Khan Academy: Data Analysis - Part of AP Computer Science Principles.
  • DataBasic.io - Web tools for beginners to explore data.
  • Science Buddies: Big Data Projects - Hands-on science projects involving big data.

Drug Discovery

In the fields of medicine, biotechnology and pharmacology, drug discovery is the process by which new candidate medications are discovered.

  • NIH: The Drug Development Process - How research turns into health solutions.
  • Nature: Drug Discovery - Latest news and research (Advanced).
  • YouTube: Drug Discovery Explained - Educational videos on the process.

Gene Therapy

Gene therapy is a medical field which focuses on the genetic modification of cells to produce a therapeutic effect or the treatment of disease by repairing or reconstructing defective genetic material.

  • MedlinePlus: What is Gene Therapy? - Accessible explanation from the National Library of Medicine.
  • ASGCT: Gene Therapy 101 - Basics from the American Society of Gene + Cell Therapy.
  • NHGRI: Gene Therapy Glossary - Definitions and context from the National Human Genome Research Institute.
